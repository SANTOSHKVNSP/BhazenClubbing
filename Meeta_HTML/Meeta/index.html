Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase tickets?

Click on the "Buy Ticket Now" button or scroll to the Pricing section. Choose between Student Pass (₹299) or General Pass (₹499) and click "Book Now" to complete your purchase securely through our ticketing partner.

How will I enter the venue?

You will receive a QR Code 2 days before the event. At the venue, show your QR Code at the entry gate to collect your wristband, which will allow you access inside.

What time should I arrive at the venue?

Gates open at 5:30 PM on January 25, 2026. We recommend arriving early to collect your wristband, find good seating, and enjoy the pre-show activities including the photo booth and merchandise stalls.

Is parking available at the venue?

Yes, parking is available at Gurajada Kalakshetram. We recommend carpooling or using public transportation as parking may be limited. Nearby parking areas and ride-sharing services are also available.

Can I get a refund if I can't attend?

All ticket sales are final. Refunds are only provided if the event is cancelled or postponed. However, you may transfer your ticket to another person by contacting our support team at least 48 hours before the event.

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

Outside food and drinks are not permitted inside the venue. Please plan accordingly and have your meals before arriving at the event.

What items are not allowed inside?

Prohibited items include: weapons, illegal substances, professional cameras with detachable lenses, outside food/drinks, laser pointers, and any items deemed dangerous. Security checks will be conducted at entry.

How can I contact support for other questions?

For any other questions, call us at +91 97030 46062 or email bhazenclubbing@gmail.com. We'll respond within 24 hours. You can also DM us on Instagram @bhazen_clubbing for quick responses.
